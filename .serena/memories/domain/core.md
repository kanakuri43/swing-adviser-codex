# Domain Layer
- `src/SwingAdviser.Domain`; dependency-free domain model. Must not reference WPF, Prism, SQLite/EF, HTTP, files, or Codex CLI.
- Owns instruments, daily bars, corporate actions, positions, MarginLot/credit contract terms, credit-cost ledger, position adjustments, executions, strategies, signals, and analysis results.
- `TechnicalIndicatorEngine` calculates PIT-verified EMA/MACD/ATR/volume evidence and returns the run/manifest/instrument/date/hash identity; `CandidateScoringEngine` applies directional MACD, strict EMA stack, and side-specific volume gates, then produces immutable, split-invariant 0-100 score components from frozen typed parameters. Strategy snapshot hashes include strategy and algorithm versions as well as the typed parameter body.
- `InitialRiskPlanBundle` (`initial-risk-plan-factory-v1`) freezes an exact effective opening revision, entry price/unit, ATR identity, and `RiskManagementParameters` (`risk-management-parameters-v1`; initial long/short stops 3.0/2.5 ATR, target 1.5R, fraction 0.50) into a `RiskBasisSnapshot` plus revision-1 initial plan. Reject mismatched lot/execution/unit data, non-positive price lines, or initial revisions with a predecessor.
- `LotRiskEvaluator` (`holding-risk-evaluation-v1`) selects the sole complete active risk-plan leaf at the UTC cutoff and applies side-specific bar high/low boundaries; invalid, cyclic, branched, disconnected, or unit-mismatched inputs fail closed, and StopLoss wins if stop and take-profit are both reached.
- `LotHoldingRiskEvaluator` shares `holding-risk-evaluation-v1` and, after current/prior take-profit reach, combines side-specific MACD crossover and close-vs-EMA20 reversal: either match yields Exit, both known non-matches yield TakeProfit, and missing reach/reversal evidence yields Indeterminate. Preserve decision priority StopLoss > Exit > TakeProfit > Hold and require prior-reach evidence to match the exact earlier bar, plan, unit, field, and boundary.
- `LotPartialExitQuantityCalculator` has no separate algorithm ID; it floors `currentQuantity * fraction` to the trading unit and returns Candidate only when both the exit and remainder are feasible whole-unit quantities, otherwise NotFeasible with no candidate/effective fraction/remainder.
- `PartialExitBreakevenPlanFactory` (`partial-exit-breakeven-plan-factory-v1`) requires the exact effective user-confirmed close/allocation, a genuinely partial close, and a directly superseding revision. Keep take-profit unchanged and never loosen the stop (long=max(previous, entry basis), short=min(previous, entry basis)); record the close/allocation as trigger evidence.
- Preserve original user-entered executions as audit originals; corporate-action conversions are separate adjustment history.
- Model unknown/unpublished/missing separately from zero or not-applicable, especially margin costs and deadlines.
